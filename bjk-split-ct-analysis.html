Split CT-Side · lt10s First-Kill Positioning

Besiktas — What Makes Them Different

Comparing BJK against 11 other teams on Split CT-side. Data from last 3 maps per team, positions captured at first-kill moment (rounds where FK happened within 10 seconds).

Two structural patterns stand out: BJK players are compressed into a narrow horizontal corridor (Finding 3), and they almost never send anyone into the far-flank early positions that other teams routinely use (Finding 2). The result: reactive rather than proactive CT play — and a 33% early FK win rate, vs 65% average across the field.

Finding 3 — Narrow horizontal corridor
Finding 3

Average X-range per round (team width)

Per-round X-range = max(normX) − min(normX) across players in that round. Measures how "wide" the team is deployed horizontally when a first kill happens.

BJK avg: 14.7 · Field avg: 20.0. BJK is the 2nd most compressed team, and the only one with a very high enemy-FK rate alongside it.

Finding 3 · Round-by-round

X-range vs Y-range per round — BJK only

Each circle = one round. X-axis = how horizontally compressed the team is (small = tight). Y-axis = how vertically spread they are (large = covering A-to-B). Good defensive setups tend to have wide X + wide Y. BJK has narrow X + wide Y — a vertical column formation.

Most circles cluster in the bottom-left quadrant: tight X-range, high Y-range. Compare: FKS/TL often reach X-range > 20 and Y-range > 65 simultaneously.
